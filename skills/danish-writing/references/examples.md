# Before/after examples

Real corrections of agent-written Danish. **These take priority over `checklist.md`.** Treat the patterns behind each correction as rules, not just the specific sentences.

Add new pairs at the bottom. Keep each one short: the sentence or paragraph that changed, not the whole text. A one-line note on *why* helps the model generalize.

## Template

```markdown
### <short label, e.g. "Status email to team">

**Before (agent):**
> …

**After (edited):**
> …

**Why:** …
```
